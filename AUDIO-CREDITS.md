# Little English Songs — sources and permissions

All three songs were composed for Little Deer English Trail. Words, note sequences, backing arrangement and final mixes are original; no existing commercial song or backing performance was imported. The vocals are locally synthesized, rhythm-aligned and pitch-shifted. They are intentionally labelled as synthetic vocals, not a human recording.

| Song | File | Content |

## Synthesis tools and acknowledgment

The local production process used the installed FFmpeg `flite` filter with the CMU Flite `slt` voice, followed by local pitch/time processing and an original synthesized instrumental arrangement. The website serves the resulting MP3s; it does not bundle Flite, FFmpeg, their source code, or a voice model, and does not call any speech-generation service.

Flite: Carnegie Mellon University, Language Technologies Institute; CMU US SLT voice, Alan W Black. No endorsement is implied.

Official sources checked during production:

- [Flite official COPYING](https://github.com/festvox/flite/blob/master/COPYING): BSD-like runtime license allowing unrestricted commercial use/distribution subject to the listed notices and no endorsement.
- [Official CMU US SLT source notice](https://github.com/festvox/flite/blob/master/lang/cmu_us_slt/cmu_us_slt.c): permissive CMU notice.
- A copy of the official Flite notice is retained in FLITE-NOTICE.txt for acknowledgment and provenance; it does not replace this project's license for original compositions and mixes.

All fish/puzzle illustrations are also original inline or local SVG artwork. Original synthesized background music from the first release remains separate from these vocal songs. Ordinary lesson Listen continues to use the device's en-US Web Speech voice.
